## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 🚨 One-Tap Emergency SOS & Priority Dispatch
* Students can specify their hostel block and room number.
* Pressing the **SOS Button** or selecting a quick trigger immediately simulates high-priority alerts to **Akash Yadav**, **Anupam Yadav**, **Aman Kumar**, and the **Campus Ambulance driver**.
* Auto-generates auditory feedback and live GPS beacon logging.

### 2. 📞 Situation-Based Emergency Directory
Categorized by emergency type with 1-tap direct dialing:
* **🚑 Medical & Hospital:** Campus Ambulance (102), Health Center Doctor on Duty, City Trauma Center, National EMS (108).
* **👮 Police & Campus Security:** Campus Security Control Room, Chief Proctor / Flying Squad, Women's Safety Helpline (1090/112), Local Police Post.
* **🔥 Fire & Hazard:** Campus Fire Safety Officer, National Fire Brigade (101).
* **🧠 Mental Health SOS:** Campus Student Wellness Counselor, Tele-MANAS (14416 / 1800-891-4416).

### 3. 🎙️ AI Voice & Text Triage Assistant
* Natural language symptom input with **speech-to-text voice recognition**.
* Classifies severity:
  * **Code Red (Critical):** Immediate CPR/airway/bleeding control + auto-dispatches ambulance.
  * **Code Purple (Security/Police):** Locks down student safety + alerts proctorial squad & police.
  * **Code Yellow (Urgent):** Food poisoning, sprains, high fever first-aid guidance.
  * **Code Green (Routine):** Dispensary visit & hydration protocol.
* **Hands-free audio instructions:** Reads out loud the step-by-step first-aid steps using browser SpeechSynthesis.

### 4. ❤️ Interactive CPR Metronome
* Built-in 110 BPM visual and audio metronome adhering to standard American Heart Association guidelines for emergency chest compressions.

### 5. 💊 Campus Dispensary Medicine Scanner
* Students or coordinators can search or scan prescription names (e.g., Paracetamol, Cetirizine, Digene, ORS, Betadine).
* Immediately shows campus pharmacy shelf location, current stock count, indications, and dosage.

### 6. 🗺️ Campus Outbreak Surveillance Heatmap
* Anonymously aggregates student symptoms across hostel blocks (Block A, B, C, D, Girls Hostel).
* Proactively flags high clusters of symptoms (e.g. food poisoning from mess water in Hostel Block C).
